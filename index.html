TIER 4 · CORRECTION + PROTECTION

Paint Correction + Ceramic

Ray's highest-level finish for restoring paint clarity and adding long-term protection.

  • Everything in Tier 3
  • Multi-stage machine paint correction
  • Swirl, light-scratch and oxidation reduction
  • Paint leveling and refining for depth, clarity and gloss
  • Professional-grade ceramic coating
  • UV, chemical and environmental protection
  • Hydrophobic finish and easier maintenance
Condition-based service: paint correction and ceramic work are confirmed after Ray evaluates the vehicle's paint and desired outcome.
